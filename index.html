No more embarrassing bursts of sound.

CornMute is a Chrome extension that mutes the tab the moment an adult / NSFW site starts loading — before a single sound can play. Unmute whenever you're ready.

  • Muted from the first second. CornMute acts when the page starts loading, not after it begins playing.
  • Works in Incognito. Chrome's built-in “Mute site” isn't remembered in Incognito. CornMute is (just allow it in Incognito once).
  • You stay in control. Unmute with one click or Alt+Shift+M. Once unmuted, the tab stays unmuted while it stays on that site.
  • Tens of thousands of adult sites built in — plus every site on the adult-only .xxx, .porn, .sex and .adult domains.
  • Your own list. Add any site under “Muted sites”. Subdomains are covered automatically.
  • Private by design. No accounts, no analytics, no data collected. Addresses are checked on your device and never stored or sent.
  • Discreet. No site names appear in Chrome's install prompt.
