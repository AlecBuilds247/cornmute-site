Privacy policy

Effective November 19, 2026 · Applies to the CornMute Chrome extension published by AlecBuilds.

The short version

CornMute collects no data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no tracking and no ads. Nothing you do is sent anywhere.

What CornMute does on your device

  • Checks addresses locally. When a tab starts loading a page, CornMute compares the site's domain with its built-in list (bundled inside the extension, never downloaded) and your own list. This happens entirely inside your browser. Page addresses are never saved and never sent to anyone.
  • Mutes or unmutes the tab. That's its only action.

What CornMute stores (only on your computer)

  • Whether CornMute is on or off.
  • The sites you added under “My sites”.
  • A single number: how many “awkward moments” were prevented.
  • While Chrome is open, which tabs CornMute muted (kept in memory and cleared when Chrome closes). Only the matching listed domain is kept — never the page address.

This data is stored with Chrome's local extension storage. It is not synced, not uploaded and not shared. Uninstalling CornMute deletes it.

What CornMute never does

  • No collection of personal information, browsing history, page content, or usage statistics.
  • No selling or transferring of data to third parties.
  • No remote code: everything CornMute runs ships inside the extension.
  • No content scripts: CornMute does not read or change web pages.

Permissions, in plain English

  • webNavigation — to know when a tab starts loading a page, so it can be muted before sound plays.
  • storage — to remember your on/off setting, your sites and the counter, on your device.
  • activeTab — when you open the CornMute popup or press its shortcut, to read the current tab's site so you can mute/unmute it or add it to your list.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
