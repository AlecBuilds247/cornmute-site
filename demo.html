Demo: a harmless test tone

This page plays a plain beep. It lets you (or a reviewer) see CornMute work without visiting any adult site.

  1. Open CornMute's popup and click Add this site — or add this site under My sites.
  2. Reload this page. The tab shows Chrome's muted speaker icon and the popup says “Muted by CornMute”.
  3. Click Play test tone. You hear nothing, and the popup's “awkward moments prevented” counter goes up.
  4. Click Unmute this tab in the popup (or press Alt+Shift+M). Now you hear the tone. Reload: it stays unmuted on this site.
